01 / Purpose

Built to support research, not replace judgment.

The application organizes public market information, rule-based technical analysis, backtesting, risk calculations, and simulated trades in a local dashboard. It is intended primarily for personal use.

The application does not execute real cryptocurrency trades. It is not a financial advisory service and does not promise investment returns.

02 / Capabilities

One research loop, six focused tools.

01

Market monitoring

Tracks public BTC, ETH, and SOL quotes and completed historical candles.

02

Technical indicators

Calculates MA20, MA50, RSI, MACD, ATR, momentum, trend, and volatility conditions.

03

Historical backtesting

Tests rule-based strategies with fees, slippage, and controls against future-data leakage.

04

Paper trading

Records simulated entries, exits, stop levels, and account performance in local storage.

05

Risk analysis

Estimates position limits, fees, loss exposure, reward-to-risk, and break-even prices.

06

Email notifications

Sends user-configured market alerts and daily investment reports through Gmail.

03 / Gmail integration

Send-only access, for notifications you configure.

gmail.send

Gmail integration is used only to send user-configured market alerts and daily investment reports. The application does not request permission to read the Gmail inbox, contacts, drafts, labels, or message history.
